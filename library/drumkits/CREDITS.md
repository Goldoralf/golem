# DrumGizmo kits — attributions

The kits themselves are **not in the repository** (2.3 GB per kit): `provision/fetch-assets.sh` downloads them.
Their licences **require attribution**: wherever golem shows credits (on screen or in an export), these lines must
appear.

## The Aasimonster v2.1

- **Authors**: the DrumGizmo team — recorded by Bent Bisballe Nyeng et al.
- **Licence**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Source**: <https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:the_aasimonster>
- **Content**: 14 microphones (+ trigger and module = 16 channels), 2 bass drums, 3 rack toms, 1 floor tom,
  2 16" crashes, 1 18" china, 2 small chinas, 1 zilbel, 1 ride.

> The licence asks that "any context where other credits are shown includes the attribution". In practice: as soon
> as a golem screen shows an author (Library, preset export, sound guide), The Aasimonster must be credited.
